How I Approach Applied AI

A few principles that guide how I build AI-assisted applications.

Backend-First Thinking

I build AI features on solid API, authentication, and data foundations rather than treating them as bolt-ons.

Retrieval Before Generation

Where possible, I ground LLM outputs in retrieved, relevant context rather than relying on the model alone.

Practical Over Novel

I favor dependable, explainable matching and retrieval techniques over unnecessarily complex architectures.
